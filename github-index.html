About us

Incorporating faith into our learning

High Point Family Day Care Center is a state-licensed childcare center in High Point, NC, serving infants, toddlers, preschoolers, and school-aged children with curriculum-based programs built on NC Foundations tools. We're a faith-based community that incorporates the teachings of Jesus into our curriculum while providing academic preparation guided by God's Word — all within a safe, loving, and fun environment that supports family involvement and every child's individual development.

Teachers as leaders

"Teachers lead by example by having positive attitudes, dynamic classroom settings, and curriculum-based lesson plans that encourage our students to be mini-leaders, allowing them to have room to express themselves freely."

Our mission

To provide a safe, structured, and engaging environment where every child builds the academic skills, faith foundation, and personal confidence to lead — in the classroom and beyond.

Opening in High Point · 2026

High Point Family Leadership Academy

Building on our early childhood foundation, we're expanding into a private school for the elementary years — with nationally accredited academics, small classes, and the same faith-based, family-first approach our community already trusts.

The High Point Family Leadership Academy extends everything families already know us for — small class sizes, consistent routines, and teachers who know every child by name — into a full academic program for Kindergarten through 2nd grade. Every lesson plan is built to encourage students to be "mini-leaders," with room to express themselves freely while building the academic skills and personal confidence to lead, in the classroom and beyond.

Tuition support is available for qualifying families through the NC Opportunity Scholarship, North Carolina's statewide private-school scholarship program.

New · Grades K – 2

Lower School

A full academic curriculum for Kindergarten, 1st, and 2nd graders — extending our Pre-K foundation into elementary-level leadership and academics, with the same dependable before- and after-care hours.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What ages and grades do you serve?
We care for children from infancy through Pre-Kindergarten, offer before- and after-school care for ages 5–12, and — through the High Point Family Leadership Academy — now enroll Kindergarten through 2nd grade in our Lower School.
When can I tour the center or academy?
Tours are available daily from 5 to 7 PM, and Saturdays by appointment. Submit the enrollment form above and we'll find a time that works for you.
What are your hours of operation?
We're open Monday through Friday, 6:30 AM to 5:30 PM, covering your child before and after the school day.
What does before & after care include?
Homework support, quiet study time, enrichment activities, and supervised play — available every school day, plus seasonal camps.
How do you keep parents updated?
Once enrolled, families receive daily updates, photos, and messages through our Brightwheel parent app, so you stay connected throughout the day.
How do I get started?
Fill out the enrollment form with a few details about your child. We'll reach out within one business day to schedule your tour.
